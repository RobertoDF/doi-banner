DOI / Link Banner

Privacy policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026. Applies to the DOI / Link Banner website (robertodf.github.io/doi-banner), the DOI / Link Banner add-in for Microsoft PowerPoint, and the Keynote helper script in the source repository.

Summary

DOI / Link Banner turns a DOI or web link into an image banner for slides. It runs entirely in your browser or in the PowerPoint task pane. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no server of ours that receives your data.

What happens to what you type

  • DOIs are sent directly from your browser to the public metadata services that answer them: Crossref (api.crossref.org), and if Crossref has no record, DataCite (api.datacite.org) and doi.org. Only the DOI is sent. Those services see your IP address like any website you visit, and they handle it under their own privacy policies.
  • Web links are not fetched or sent anywhere. The banner and its QR code are built locally from the text you entered.
  • Edited text, settings and generated images stay in the page's memory. They are discarded when you close the page or task pane. Nothing is written to cookies or local storage.

PowerPoint add-in

  • The add-in requests permission to write to the document. It uses this only when you press Insert into slide, to place the banner image at your current selection. It does not read your presentation's content.
  • Microsoft's Office JavaScript library is loaded from Microsoft's CDN (appsforoffice.microsoft.com), as required for all Office add-ins. Its use is covered by the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Clipboard, downloads and the Keynote script

Copy image and the download buttons only act when you click them. The image goes to your clipboard or your downloads folder. The optional Keynote AppleScript runs on your Mac: it reads the image from your clipboard and places it on the current Keynote slide, and sends nothing over the network.

Hosting

The website and the add-in's pages are static files hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses when serving them; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have no access to those logs.

Children

The service collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page and in the repository history. Questions go to support.
